# Large Event Catering Guide - Gambian Delights

## Overview
This guide provides detailed information for handling large events (100-200+ people) including menu planning, pricing, logistics, and best practices.

---

## Pricing Structure for Large Events

### Tier 1: 100-120 People
- **Price per person:** 85-90 kr
- **Discount:** 5% bulk discount
- **Menu:** 2-3 main dishes, sides, drinks
- **Total cost example:** 100 people × 87.50 kr = **8,750 kr**

### Tier 2: 121-150 People (Most Popular)
- **Price per person:** 80-85 kr
- **Discount:** 10% bulk discount
- **Menu:** 3-4 main dishes, multiple sides, beverage options
- **Total cost example:** 150 people × 82.50 kr = **12,375 kr**

### Tier 3: 151-200+ People
- **Price per person:** 75-80 kr
- **Discount:** 15% bulk discount
- **Menu:** Full menu selection, premium service, custom options
- **Total cost example:** 200 people × 77.50 kr = **15,500 kr**

## Portion Planning Guide

### Main Dishes
- **Per person:** 150-200g of each main dish
- **Buffer:** Add 10-15% extra for unexpected guests
- **Example for 150 people:**
  - Benachin: 150 × 175g = 26.25 kg (order 30 kg)
  - Domoda: 150 × 175g = 26.25 kg (order 30 kg)
  - Yassa: 150 × 175g = 26.25 kg (order 30 kg)

### Sides
- **Plantain:** 50-75g per person
- **Salad:** 50-75g per person
- **Extra Sauce:** 30-50ml per person

### Drinks
- **Fresh Juice:** 1-2 servings per person
- **Soft Drinks:** 1-2 servings per person
- **Water:** 2-3 servings per person

## Logistics & Timing

### Booking Timeline
1. **14+ days before event:** Initial booking and deposit (50%)
2. **7 days before:** Menu confirmation and final guest count estimate
3. **3 days before:** Final guest count confirmation and remaining payment (50%)
4. **1 day before:** Final logistics check (delivery time, location, setup)

### Delivery & Setup
- **Delivery time:** Coordinate 1-2 hours before serving time
- **Setup time:** Allow 30-60 minutes for setup
- **Equipment needed:**
  - Serving tables/station
  - Chafing dishes or warming equipment
  - Serving utensils
  - Plates, cutlery (if not provided by venue)
  - Napkins, cups

### Storage Requirements
- **Hot food:** Must be kept above 60°C
- **Cold food:** Must be kept below 4°C
- **Reheating:** If needed, reheat to 75°C minimum

## Tips for Success

### For the Business (You)
1. **Plan ahead:** Start prep 2-3 days before large events
2. **Batch cooking:** Cook in large batches for efficiency
3. **Quality control:** Taste test each batch
4. **Staff planning:** Consider extra help for events 150+
5. **Equipment check:** Ensure you have enough pots, pans, storage
6. **Backup plan:** Have backup ingredients and equipment ready

### For the Customer
1. **Book early:** Minimum 14 days notice required
2. **Confirm numbers:** Final count 3 days before
3. **Dietary needs:** Provide dietary requirements list early
4. **Venue prep:** Ensure adequate space and equipment
5. **Timing:** Plan serving time realistically
6. **Communication:** Stay in touch with updates

## Dietary Accommodations

### Common Requests
- **Vegan:** Can accommodate (use vegetable stock, plant-based ingredients)
- **Gluten-free:** Most dishes naturally gluten-free
- **Halal:** Can prepare halal versions
- **Allergies:** Must be informed in advance

### Planning for Dietary Needs
- Ask for dietary requirements when booking
- Plan 10-15% of portions for special dietary needs
- Label all dishes clearly
- Keep separate serving utensils for allergen-free dishes

## Payment Terms for Large Events

### Deposit
- **50% deposit** required at booking
- **Non-refundable** if cancelled less than 5 days before

### Final Payment
- **50% balance** due 3 days before event
- **Payment methods:** Klarna, Visa, Mastercard, Vipps

### Cancellation Policy
- **More than 5 days:** Full deposit refund (minus 10% fee)
- **3-5 days:** Deposit retained, can transfer to new date
- **Less than 3 days:** Deposit + 30% of balance forfeited

## Common Challenges & Solutions

### Challenge: Last-minute guest count changes
**Solution:** Build 10-15% buffer into all orders, have flexible pricing

### Challenge: Dietary restrictions
**Solution:** Always ask early, prepare separate batches if needed

### Challenge: Delivery timing
**Solution:** Coordinate clearly, have backup transport, plan for traffic

### Challenge: Food temperature
**Solution:** Use proper warming equipment, deliver close to serving time

### Challenge: Space constraints
**Solution:** Discuss venue setup early, recommend buffet-style service
