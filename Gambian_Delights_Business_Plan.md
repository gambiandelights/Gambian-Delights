### Food Safety Steps

1. **Handwashing:** Wash hands before handling food, after touching raw meat, after using restroom
2. **Separate Raw/Cooked:** Use separate cutting boards and utensils for raw and cooked foods
3. **Temperature Control:**
   - Cook meat to 75°C internal temperature
   - Keep hot food above 63°C
   - Keep cold food below 5°C
   - Cool food quickly (within 90 minutes to below 8°C)
4. **Storage:** Label and date all prepared foods, use FIFO (First In, First Out)
5. **Cleaning:** Clean and sanitize surfaces after each use, deep clean weekly

## 6. Kitchen Workflow & Staff

### Cooking Flow

```
PREP (Morning)
  ↓
  - Wash and cut vegetables
  - Marinate meat/fish
  - Prepare sauces and bases
  - Set up packaging station
  ↓
COOK (Based on Orders)
  ↓
  - Cook main dishes
  - Prepare sides
  - Keep hot until service
  ↓
PACK (Order Time)
  ↓
  - Portion control
  - Package in containers
  - Add labels/stickers
  - Include reheating instructions
  ↓
DELIVER/PICKUP
  ↓
  - Delivery: Package for transport
```

- [ ] Check portion sizes
- [ ] Verify temperature (hot dishes hot, cold dishes cold)
- [ ] Check appearance (presentation, color)

### Complaint Handling

**Process:**
1. **Listen:** Let customer explain fully
2. **Apologize:** "I'm sorry to hear that, let me make it right"
3. **Fix Immediately:**
   - Offer replacement dish
   - Offer full/partial refund
   - Offer free side on next order
4. **Follow Up:** Check in next day to ensure satisfaction
5. **Learn:** Document issue and prevent recurrence

**Example Responses:**
- "I'm so sorry the food wasn't to your liking. I'd like to send you a replacement/refund. What would work best?"
- "Thank you for letting me know. I'll make sure this doesn't happen again. Here's a voucher for your next order."
