## Pricing Formula for Longevity

### The 3x Rule (Recommended for Food Service)

**Selling Price = (Ingredient Cost × 3) + Labor + Overhead**

**Breakdown:**
- **33%** = Ingredient costs
- **33%** = Labor & time
- **33%** = Overhead (utilities, packaging, marketing, profit)

### Alternative: Cost-Plus Pricing

**Selling Price = Total Cost × (1 + Desired Profit Margin)**

**For sustainable business:**
- **Minimum profit margin:** 60-65%
- **Ideal profit margin:** 68-75%
- **Premium margin (specialty items):** 75-80%

- Good profit margins (68-74%)
- Competitive in Norwegian market
- Allows for occasional discounts/promotions
- Sustainable for long-term growth

### 2. **Volume Strategy**
- **Catering orders:** Offer 5-10% discount for orders over 10 portions

### 3. **Upsell Strategy** (High Profit Items)
Focus on selling:
- **Sides** (80-87% margin) - Plantain, Salad, Sauce
- **Drinks** (70-80% margin) - Juice, Soft drinks
- **Combos** - Main + Side + Drink = Better value perception

### 4. **Cost Control**
To maintain profitability:
- **Buy in bulk** when possible (10-15% savings)
- **Seasonal ingredients** - adjust menu based on prices
- **Efficient prep** - batch cooking saves time/labor
- **Minimize waste** - plan portions carefully
